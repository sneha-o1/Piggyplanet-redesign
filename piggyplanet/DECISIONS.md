# DECISIONS

## 1. Track and approach

I chose **Part 2 — The Premium Home Page** rather than Part 1 — the ingestion/scraper track. The assignment asks candidates to pick one track and go deep, so I chose the track where I could demonstrate the strongest product and UI work within the available time.

PiggyPlanet was an existing product, which gave me a realistic foundation to redesign rather than creating a superficial concept from scratch. I focused the implementation on making the product feel shipped: a clear hero value proposition, strong call to action, interactive product preview, saving journey, Daily Surprise interaction, responsive behavior, and restrained motion.

For the product preview, I used clearly labelled illustrative/demo data. I intentionally avoided fabricated testimonials, user counts, reviews, or company claims.

## 2. Trade-off

The main trade-off was **breadth versus polish**. Instead of adding many sections and interactions, I focused on a smaller set of experiences and made them cohesive: the hero, product preview, saving journey, Daily Surprise interaction, How It Works section, and final CTA.

With a real week, I would extend the product preview into a deeper interactive prototype, perform broader accessibility and cross-browser testing, test with more devices, and refine the animation timing based on real user feedback.

## 3. AI usage and verification

I used **AI tools, including ChatGPT**, for brainstorming, implementation assistance, debugging, and reviewing UI and responsive-design decisions.

I personally integrated and reviewed the generated code within the existing Angular/Ionic project. I verified the production build, tested the deployed page in the browser, checked responsiveness at **390px mobile and 1440px desktop widths**, and tested navigation, animations, the Daily Surprise interaction, and the landing-page flow.

I also reviewed the content specifically for fabricated claims and changed the presentation to use clearly labelled demo data. The final decisions about the visual direction, dark theme, layout, animation usage, interaction design, and product presentation were made and reviewed by me.
